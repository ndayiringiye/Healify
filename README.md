# Healify 🏥🤖

## AI-Powered Hospital Management & Smart Healthcare Innovation Platform

Healify is a modern healthcare technology platform designed to transform hospital management through Artificial Intelligence (AI), Internet of Things (IoT), and intelligent digital healthcare solutions.

The platform helps hospitals, doctors, patients, and administrators manage healthcare services efficiently while enabling smart monitoring, automation, and real-time healthcare innovation.

---

# 🌍 Vision

Healify aims to revolutionize healthcare systems by building a secure, scalable, intelligent, and connected healthcare ecosystem powered by AI and IoT technologies.
